## 🌿 The Murrwood

At the very heart stands the Great Tree of Fate — the source of power and balance of the entire Kingdom of Meow. As long as it lives — the world holds together.

But peace has been broken. The Ogre Dogs are advancing, and the Tree is under threat.

Now everything rests in your paws. Keep the balance… or let the world fall.

But be careful… Deep within the depths, something lurks — something whose name is never spoken aloud.

Murrwood's fate now rests in your paws. Protect it well.

## 🎮 About the Project

The Murrwood is a project developed by XIII Pixels as part of the Lesta Academy program.

This build showcases the core gameplay mechanics, art style, and the world
